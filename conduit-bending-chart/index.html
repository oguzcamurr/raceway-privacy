Offset multiplier and shrink chart

Distance between bends = offset depth × multiplier. Shrink = offset depth × shrink per inch. Multiplier = 1 ÷ sin θ (the cosecant); shrink per inch = (1 − cos θ) ÷ sin θ, which equals tan(θ/2).

About the numbers: these calculators use the point model behind apprenticeship charts — each bend is treated as a sharp corner. Real benders have a radius and some springback, which shift the marks slightly. Bend and measure a test piece from scrap before you bend a run. This page uses no electrical-code data.

Common questions

What is the multiplier for a 22.5 degree bend?

2.613 (1 ÷ sin 22.5°); charts round it to 2.6. The shrink is 0.199 inch per inch of offset depth, usually written as 3/16 inch.

What is the multiplier for a 15 degree offset?

3.864 (1 ÷ sin 15°), about 3.9 rounded to one decimal. The shrink is 0.132 inch per inch of offset depth, about 1/8 inch.

What is the multiplier for a 10 degree offset?

5.759 (1 ÷ sin 10°); most charts and benders use 6. The shrink is 0.087 inch per inch of offset depth; charts list 1/16 inch.

What is the multiplier for a 45 degree offset?

1.414 (1 ÷ sin 45°); charts use 1.4. The shrink is 0.414 inch per inch of offset depth, about 3/8 inch.
