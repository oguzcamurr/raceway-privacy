Raceway — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 (updated for Angle Finder, build 1.0 (2)) App: Raceway (iOS) · Developer: Oguz Camur Contact: oguzcamuur@outlook.com

Summary

Raceway does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network features. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Raceway stores, and where

  • Calculations, saved results and settings. Kept only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Bender profiles and calibration measurements you enter. Kept only on your device.
  • Reference values you type in (for example code values or spec-sheet dimensions). Kept only on your device.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it in Settings → "Erase all Raceway data". Like other app data, it can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your device, under your Apple backup settings.

Camera, photos and motion sensors (Angle Finder)

  • Camera. Raceway uses the camera only when you tap "Take photo" in Angle Finder, and iOS asks your permission first. The photo is used on your device to measure a bend angle. Raceway does not save it to your photo library or to the app's storage, and it is discarded when you leave the screen.
  • Choosing a photo. "Choose photo" uses the iOS photo picker, which gives Raceway only the photo you pick and no other access to your library. The photo is not copied or stored.
  • Motion sensors. The live angle meter reads your iPhone's motion sensors while the meter is open, to show the phone's tilt. These readings are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • If you save a measurement, Raceway keeps only the angle and whether it came from the meter or a photo.

Raceway does not ask for access to your location, contacts, microphone or any other protected data.

What Raceway sends

Nothing. Raceway makes no network requests. It contains no third-party software development kits, analytics or advertising code.

Files you choose to export or import

If you use "Export reference pack", Raceway creates a file and asks you where to save it using the iOS file picker. Where that file goes, and who you share it with, is your choice. "Import reference pack" reads only the file you pick.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test Raceway through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports, usage data and any feedback or screenshots you choose to submit, under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer receives that information through App Store Connect and uses it only to fix and improve Raceway.

Children

Raceway is a professional tool for electrical workers. It is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
