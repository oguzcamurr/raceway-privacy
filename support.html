Raceway: Conduit Bending is an offline iOS calculation aid for electricians: conduit bending marks corrected for your own bender, plus voltage drop.

Getting help

Email oguzcamuur@outlook.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and Raceway version (Settings in the app), and a screenshot if you can. Beta testers can also use Share Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

Important

  • Bending results are not yet field-validated. Bend and measure a test piece from scrap before bending a run.
  • Raceway is a calculation aid, not a code-compliance decision. Check your code book and your authority having jurisdiction (AHJ).
  • Raceway stores everything on your device and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
